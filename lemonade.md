# Lemonade by Prashanthi

## Ingredients
- 4 lemons
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 4 cups cold water
- Ice cubes
- Mint leaves (optional)

## Instructions
1. Squeeze the lemons to get lemon juice.
2. In a pitcher, mix sugar and 1 cup warm water until sugar dissolves.
3. Add lemon juice and 3 cups cold water. Stir well.
4. Add ice cubes and mint leaves.
5. Serve cold and enjoy!

## Notes
- Adjust sugar as per taste.
- You can add a pinch of salt for extra flavour.
